# ESP32-S3 specificities

Tests were conducted on an ESP32-RS485-CAN, some other boards are likely compatible. They could be listed here. The sole difference will be the pinout for connecting to the RTU port.

Waveshare wiki for reference: https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-RS485-CAN

Schematics: https://files.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-RS485-CAN/ESP32-S3-RS485-CAN-Schematic.pdf

Pinout:
- GPIO17: TXD1
- GPIO18: RXD1
- GPIO21: EN
